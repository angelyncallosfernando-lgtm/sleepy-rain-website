About Us

Sleepy Rain Sanctuary is an independent digital content project dedicated to creating peaceful ambient experiences.

Our content combines AI-generated artwork, animated rain effects and procedurally synthesized audio to create relaxing virtual environments.

Our Soundscapes

  • Peaceful forest rain
  • Heavy rainfall
  • Midnight storms
  • Gentle and moderate rain
  • Rain with wind
  • Rain with distant thunder-inspired sounds

Our YouTube Integration

Sleepy Rain Sanctuary uses the YouTube Data API to upload generated videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to its own authorized YouTube channel.

This integration is a private administrative tool operated by the channel owner. Videos are uploaded privately for manual review before publication.

The application is not a public YouTube account management service.
